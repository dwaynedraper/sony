## MAIN — email / huddle

**Subject: The store trackers are back — and they don't want anything about you**

I've had more time to sit with the store trackers, and I found a way to keep your history
without collecting **any** of your information.

**Gone:** emails, names, addresses, geolocation. All of it.

Instead, it asks for your **schedule**, once — Sun = 0058, Mon = 0148, and so on. Everything then
saves against the **store number**, not against you. The app has no idea who you are, and it
doesn't need to.

The honest details:

- **There's no login anymore at all**, so there's nothing to sign back into. Set your schedule once
  and you're done.
- You'll enter it **once per device**, and again if you clear your cache. That's the trade for not
  having an account, and I think it's the right one.
- **You can delete your cookies safely — the app doesn't use any.** Not one.
- Your schedule is stored in localStorage, which means **it never leaves your device.** The only
  thing that syncs is the *store's* data — out-of-stocks, display issues, layout — and that's tied
  to a store number, never to a person. That's how you get history without me knowing a thing
  about you.

**It's also laid out like the actual display now.** A top-down view of the table, so you tap where
the thing physically sits. And if your store doesn't follow the planogram — plenty don't — you can
**edit your store's layout** to match your real table. That change is yours alone; it never touches
another store.

You can also **toggle between Out of Stock and the Display Issue Tracker** right where you are,
without backing out and starting over.

I watched usage fall off a cliff after I added the login, and that one's on me. **I was trying to
persist store data, not collect your data** — but I understand why it looked like the second one,
and I'm sorry to those of you it scared off.

It's faster, it's simpler, and it doesn't ask you for a single thing about yourself. I hope you'll
give it another shot — and I hope a lot more of you find it useful this time.

👉 [LINK]

Anything broken or annoying, tell me and I'll fix it.

— Dean
