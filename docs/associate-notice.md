## LONG — email / huddle

**Subject: The Sony toolkit is back — no login, no email, no tracking**

Hey all,

A bunch of you bailed on the toolkit last time, and honestly you were right to. I tied it to
email logins and personal accounts, and nobody wants to hand over personal info to use a survey
tool. That was my mistake, and I've rebuilt it.

**What's gone — permanently:**

- No email. No login. No password. No magic links.
- No geolocation. It never asks where you are, and it never could.
- No personal data of any kind. There's nothing in the database with your name on it.

**How it works now:** the app is built around your **store number**, not around you. Everything is
tied to the store — the out-of-stock list, the display issues, the table layout. Not to a person.

**Set your schedule once.** Punch in which store you work each day of the week (mine is 0180
Friday–Saturday, 0058 Sunday, 0180 Monday–Tuesday). After that the app just opens to the right
store every day. It lives on your phone, not on a server.

**Swapped shifts?** Change it manually anytime — enter any store number and go. Your schedule isn't
locked to anything.

**Your table is your table.** Every store's area is organized differently, and half of them don't
match the planogram anyway. So **each store gets its own custom layout.** If your ZV cameras are on
the wrong side, or you've got an extra body where a display box should be, just edit it. It saves
for your store and it doesn't touch anyone else's. Edit it once, and every future stock check and
display survey matches what's actually in front of you.

**What you can do with it:**

- **Out of Stock** — walk the table, tap what's missing, generate a ready-to-paste list.
- **Display Issues** — flag anything alarmed, dead, broken, or missing. Same table view.
- **Camera Finder** — answer a few questions, get the right body for the customer.
- **Spec Filter** — filter and sort every camera by MP, burst, IBIS, video, sealing.
- **Basket Builder** — tell it what they bought and what they shoot, get an honest accessory basket.
- **Cage Fights** — Sony vs. the competitor, with the specs and the pitch.

It's mobile-first, because that's where you'll use it — standing at the table with one hand full.
